How to Explore The Webb

Welcome to The Webb! Explore the universe through discoveries and images captured by the James Webb Space Telescope. Follow the timeline and click any dot to uncover an astronomical image, its title, and the story behind it. Hover over a dot to see its date and find discoveries from different years. Each milestone offers a glimpse into distant galaxies, nebulae, stars, and other fascinating objects in our universe. You can also use the ★ button to discover a random NASA image once that feature is available. To close this guide or a discovery popup, click the X button or click outside the popup. Take your time, explore the discoveries, and enjoy your journey through the universe!
